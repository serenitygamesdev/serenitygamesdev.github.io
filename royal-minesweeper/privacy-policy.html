Privacy Policy — Royal Minesweeper

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Royal Minesweeper ("the app") is an offline puzzle game. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data the app stores

Your game statistics (games played and won, best times, streaks), settings and chosen theme are stored only on your device. We have no servers and never receive this data (usage analytics, described below, is separate). Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded ads (for hints and to continue after hitting a mine) and occasional full-screen ads between games. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • the device's advertising ID,
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version),
  • approximate location derived from the IP address,
  • ad interactions (views, clicks).

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and wherever required by law, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Android settings.

How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

After you accept the Terms of Use, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played, so we can improve it. It records app usage events such as screens opened, games started and finished (difficulty, result, duration, hints used), themes chosen and rewarded ads watched, together with device and app information (model, OS version, language, country, app version) and an app-instance identifier. It does not record your name, email or other contact details. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, your choices in Google's consent form also apply to this data. Google processes it on our behalf: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Leaderboards (Google Play Games)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the app submits your winning times (best time per difficulty and your daily challenge time) to Google Play Games leaderboards. Other players can see these scores together with your Play Games profile name and avatar. Sign-in, profiles and leaderboards are provided by Google; you can sign out or change your profile visibility in the Google Play Games settings on your device. The game works fully without signing in. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Purchases

The app has no in-app purchases.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
