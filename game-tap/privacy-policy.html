Game Tap – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026

Game Tap is a mobile game made by Serenity Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and what choices you have. The game is meant for players aged 13 and over.

What we do not collect

The game does not ask for your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. You do not need an account to play; signing in to Google Play Games is optional.

Information stored on your phone

Your settings (language, sound, vibration) and a copy of your game progress are saved on your phone. Your player name, chosen avatar and chosen skins are saved on your phone and on our servers, so that a saved game can bring them back on another phone.

Information stored on our servers

To keep your progress safe and to make the game fair, the game signs in anonymously with Google Firebase. This creates a random player ID. With this ID our servers keep:

  • game progress: coins, gems, owned planes, skaters and avatars, unlocked games, best scores and levels, missions and special-event results;
  • information needed to prevent cheating: results of recent runs (score, length of the run, coins collected), how many reward ads were watched today, and the total time played;
  • technical information that Firebase App Check and Google Play Integrity use to confirm that requests come from the genuine game on a genuine device.

Records of single runs and watched ads are deleted automatically after 30 days. The servers are operated by Google Cloud in the European Union (Belgium).

Google Play Games

If you sign in to Google Play Games, the game is saved to that account, so you can get it back on another phone or after reinstalling. The game then links your player ID with your Google Play Games player ID (through Firebase Authentication). We do not receive your email address or contacts. Google Play Games itself is covered by Google's Privacy Policy. If you never sign in, the game stays anonymous.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: short ads between runs, and optional ads you can choose to watch for a reward. Ads are limited to content suitable for teens. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the game first shows Google's consent form, and you choose whether ads may be personalized; you can change that choice at any time with Ad settings in the game's settings. Without consent you see only non-personalized ads.

When the law requires it (for example in the EU, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time with the Ad settings button in the game's settings.

AdMob may process device information, such as the advertising ID, IP address and app usage, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. When you watch a reward ad, AdMob sends our server a confirmation containing your player ID, so that the reward can be given. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Service providers

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Functions, Cloud Firestore, Remote Config, App Check) – storing progress and running the game's server. Firebase privacy
  • Google AdMob and the User Messaging Platform – ads and ad consent.
  • Google Play – app distribution, Play Integrity checks and the optional Google Play Games sign-in.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone else.

Deleting your data and account

In the game, open Settings and choose Delete local data. This erases the data on your phone and the data linked to your player ID on our servers. If the phone is offline, the server data is deleted the next time the game connects. Uninstalling the game removes the data on the phone; you can also contact us to have server data deleted.

Deleting your account without the game: send an email to gametap.app@gmail.com with the subject "Game Tap account deletion". If you signed in to Google Play Games, include your Play Games player name. We delete your player account and all data linked to it on our servers within 30 days, and confirm by email. Records of runs and watched ads are deleted automatically after 30 days in any case.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you are a parent and believe your child has shared personal information with us, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to its processing, or to complain to a data protection authority. Because the data is linked to a player ID rather than your name, the easiest way to delete it is the in-game option above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Game Tap – Politika privatnosti

Važi od: 20. novembra 2026.

Game Tap je igra za mobilne telefone koju je napravio Serenity Games („mi"). Ova politika objašnjava koje podatke igra koristi, zašto, i kakve izbore imaš. Igra je namenjena igračima od 13 godina i starijim.

Šta ne prikupljamo

Igra ne traži pravo ime, email adresu, broj telefona, kontakte, fotografije ni tačnu lokaciju. Za igranje nije potreban nalog, a prijava na Google Play Igre nije obavezna.

Podaci na tvom telefonu

Podešavanja (jezik, zvuk, vibracija) i kopija napretka u igri čuvaju se na telefonu. Ime igrača, izabrani avatar i izabrani skinovi čuvaju se na telefonu i na našim serverima, da bi ih sačuvana igra vratila i na drugom telefonu.

Podaci na našim serverima

Da bi napredak bio sačuvan i igra bila fer, igra se anonimno prijavljuje preko Google Firebase-a. Tako nastaje nasumičan ID igrača. Uz taj ID naši serveri čuvaju:

  • napredak u igri: novčiće, dijamante, kupljene avione, skejtere i avatare, otključane igre, najbolje rezultate i nivoe, misije i rezultate specijalnih događaja;
  • podatke potrebne za sprečavanje varanja: rezultate nedavnih partija (poeni, trajanje, skupljeni novčići), broj reklama za nagradu odgledanih danas i ukupno vreme igranja;
  • tehničke podatke koje Firebase App Check i Google Play Integrity koriste da potvrde da zahtevi dolaze iz prave igre na pravom uređaju.

Zapisi pojedinačnih partija i odgledanih reklama brišu se automatski posle 30 dana. Serveri su Google Cloud serveri u Evropskoj uniji (Belgija).

Google Play Igre

Ako se prijaviš na Google Play Igre, igra se čuva na tom nalogu, pa je možeš vratiti na drugom telefonu ili posle ponovne instalacije. Igra tada povezuje tvoj ID igrača sa tvojim ID-jem igrača u Google Play Igrama (preko Firebase Authentication). Ne dobijamo tvoju email adresu ni kontakte. Za same Google Play Igre važi Google-ova politika privatnosti. Ako se nikad ne prijaviš, igra ostaje anonimna.

Reklame

Igra je besplatna i prikazuje reklame preko Google AdMob-a: kratke reklame između partija i reklame po izboru, koje možeš da pogledaš za nagradu. Reklame su ograničene na sadržaj prikladan za tinejdžere. Tamo gde zakon to traži (na primer u Evropskom ekonomskom prostoru, Ujedinjenom Kraljevstvu i Švajcarskoj), igra prvo prikazuje Google-ov obrazac za saglasnost, a ti biraš da li reklame smeju da budu personalizovane; izbor možeš da promeniš kad god hoćeš dugmetom Podešavanja reklama u podešavanjima igre. Bez saglasnosti vidiš samo nepersonalizovane reklame.

Gde zakon to traži (na primer u EU, Velikoj Britaniji i Švajcarskoj), igra pre bilo kakve reklame traži saglasnost kroz Google-ov obrazac. Izbor možeš da promeniš kad god hoćeš dugmetom Podešavanja reklama u podešavanjima igre.

AdMob može da obrađuje podatke o uređaju, kao što su reklamni ID, IP adresa i korišćenje aplikacije, da bi prikazao i merio reklame i sprečio prevare. Kada pogledaš reklamu za nagradu, AdMob našem serveru šalje potvrdu sa tvojim ID-jem igrača, da bi nagrada mogla da se dodeli. Više u dokumentima Kako Google koristi podatke sa sajtova i aplikacija i Google-ova politika privatnosti.

Pružaoci usluga

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Functions, Cloud Firestore, Remote Config, App Check) – čuvanje napretka i rad servera igre. Firebase privatnost
  • Google AdMob i User Messaging Platform – reklame i saglasnost za reklame.
  • Google Play – distribucija aplikacije, Play Integrity provere i opcionalna prijava na Google Play Igre.

Tvoje podatke ne prodajemo i ne delimo ni sa kim drugim.

Brisanje podataka i naloga

U igri otvori Podešavanja i izaberi Obriši lokalne podatke. Time se brišu podaci na telefonu i podaci vezani za tvoj ID igrača na našim serverima. Ako telefon nije na internetu, podaci na serveru se brišu kad se igra sledeći put poveže. Deinstalacija igre briše podatke na telefonu; za brisanje podataka na serveru možeš i da nam se javiš.

Brisanje naloga bez igre: pošalji email na gametap.app@gmail.com sa naslovom „Game Tap brisanje naloga". Ako si se prijavio na Google Play Igre, navedi svoje ime igrača u Play Igrama. Nalog igrača i sve podatke vezane za njega brišemo sa naših servera u roku od 30 dana i to ti potvrđujemo emailom. Zapisi partija i odgledanih reklama se ionako sami brišu posle 30 dana.

Tvoja prava

U zavisnosti od toga gde živiš, možeš imati pravo na pristup, ispravku ili brisanje podataka, na prigovor na obradu, ili na žalbu organu za zaštitu podataka. Pošto su podaci vezani za ID igrača, a ne za tvoje ime, najlakše ih je obrisati opcijom u igri, opisanom gore.
