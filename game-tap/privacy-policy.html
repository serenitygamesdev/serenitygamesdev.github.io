Game Tap – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026

Game Tap is a mobile game made by Serenity Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and what choices you have. The game is meant for players of all ages.

What we do not collect

The game does not ask for your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. You do not need an account to play.

Information stored on your phone

Your player name, chosen avatar, settings (language, sound, vibration) and a copy of your game progress are saved only on your phone. The player name is not sent to our servers.

Information stored on our servers

To keep your progress safe and to make the game fair, the game signs in anonymously with Google Firebase. This creates a random player ID that is not linked to your name or Google account. With this ID our servers keep:

  • game progress: coins, gems, owned planes, skaters and avatars, unlocked games, best scores and levels, missions and special-event results;
  • information needed to prevent cheating: results of recent runs (score, length of the run, coins collected), how many reward ads were watched today, and the total time played;
  • technical information that Firebase App Check and Google Play Integrity use to confirm that requests come from the genuine game on a genuine device.

Records of single runs and watched ads are deleted automatically after 30 days. The servers are operated by Google Cloud in the European Union (Belgium).

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: short ads between runs, and optional ads you can choose to watch for a reward. Because the game is for all ages, every ad request is marked as directed to children and to users under the age of consent, and ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences. In this mode AdMob does not show personalized ads.

When the law requires it (for example in the EU, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time with the Ad settings button in the game's settings.

AdMob may process device information, such as the advertising ID, IP address and app usage, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. When you watch a reward ad, AdMob sends our server a confirmation containing your anonymous player ID, so that the reward can be given. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Service providers

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Functions, Cloud Firestore, Remote Config, App Check) – storing progress and running the game's server. Firebase privacy
  • Google AdMob and the User Messaging Platform – ads and ad consent.
  • Google Play – app distribution and Play Integrity checks.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone else.

Deleting your data

In the game, open Settings and choose Delete local data. This erases the data on your phone and the data linked to your anonymous player ID on our servers. If the phone is offline, the server data is deleted the next time the game connects. Uninstalling the game removes the data on the phone; you can also contact us to have server data deleted.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game works without personal information, and all ads are requested in the child-directed, non-personalized mode described above. If you are a parent and believe your child has shared personal information with us, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to its processing, or to complain to a data protection authority. Because the data is linked only to an anonymous ID, the easiest way to delete it is the in-game option above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Game Tap – Politika privatnosti

Važi od: 18. novembra 2026.

Game Tap je igra za mobilne telefone koju je napravio Serenity Games („mi"). Ova politika objašnjava koje podatke igra koristi, zašto, i kakve izbore imaš. Igra je namenjena igračima svih uzrasta.

Šta ne prikupljamo

Igra ne traži pravo ime, email adresu, broj telefona, kontakte, fotografije ni tačnu lokaciju. Za igranje nije potreban nalog.

Podaci na tvom telefonu

Ime igrača, izabrani avatar, podešavanja (jezik, zvuk, vibracija) i kopija napretka u igri čuvaju se samo na telefonu. Ime igrača se ne šalje na naše servere.

Podaci na našim serverima

Da bi napredak bio sačuvan i igra bila fer, igra se anonimno prijavljuje preko Google Firebase-a. Tako nastaje nasumičan ID igrača koji nije povezan sa tvojim imenom ni Google nalogom. Uz taj ID naši serveri čuvaju:

  • napredak u igri: novčiće, dijamante, kupljene avione, skejtere i avatare, otključane igre, najbolje rezultate i nivoe, misije i rezultate specijalnih događaja;
  • podatke potrebne za sprečavanje varanja: rezultate nedavnih partija (poeni, trajanje, skupljeni novčići), broj reklama za nagradu odgledanih danas i ukupno vreme igranja;
  • tehničke podatke koje Firebase App Check i Google Play Integrity koriste da potvrde da zahtevi dolaze iz prave igre na pravom uređaju.

Zapisi pojedinačnih partija i odgledanih reklama brišu se automatski posle 30 dana. Serveri su Google Cloud serveri u Evropskoj uniji (Belgija).

Reklame

Igra je besplatna i prikazuje reklame preko Google AdMob-a: kratke reklame između partija i reklame po izboru, koje možeš da pogledaš za nagradu. Pošto je igra za sve uzraste, svaki zahtev za reklamu označen je kao namenjen deci i korisnicima mlađim od uzrasta za saglasnost, a reklame su ograničene na sadržaj prikladan za sve. U tom režimu AdMob ne prikazuje personalizovane reklame.

Gde zakon to traži (na primer u EU, Velikoj Britaniji i Švajcarskoj), igra pre bilo kakve reklame traži saglasnost kroz Google-ov obrazac. Izbor možeš da promeniš kad god hoćeš dugmetom Podešavanja reklama u podešavanjima igre.

AdMob može da obrađuje podatke o uređaju, kao što su reklamni ID, IP adresa i korišćenje aplikacije, da bi prikazao i merio reklame i sprečio prevare. Kada pogledaš reklamu za nagradu, AdMob našem serveru šalje potvrdu sa tvojim anonimnim ID-jem igrača, da bi nagrada mogla da se dodeli. Više u dokumentima Kako Google koristi podatke sa sajtova i aplikacija i Google-ova politika privatnosti.

Pružaoci usluga

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Functions, Cloud Firestore, Remote Config, App Check) – čuvanje napretka i rad servera igre. Firebase privatnost
  • Google AdMob i User Messaging Platform – reklame i saglasnost za reklame.
  • Google Play – distribucija aplikacije i Play Integrity provere.

Tvoje podatke ne prodajemo i ne delimo ni sa kim drugim.

Brisanje podataka

U igri otvori Podešavanja i izaberi Obriši lokalne podatke. Time se brišu podaci na telefonu i podaci vezani za tvoj anonimni ID na našim serverima. Ako telefon nije na internetu, podaci na serveru se brišu kad se igra sledeći put poveže. Deinstalacija igre briše podatke na telefonu; za brisanje podataka na serveru možeš i da nam se javiš.

Deca

Svesno ne prikupljamo lične podatke dece. Igra radi bez ličnih podataka, a sve reklame se traže u režimu za decu, bez personalizacije, kako je gore opisano. Ako si roditelj i misliš da nam je dete poslalo lične podatke, javi nam se i obrisaćemo ih.

Tvoja prava

U zavisnosti od toga gde živiš, možeš imati pravo na pristup, ispravku ili brisanje podataka, na prigovor na obradu, ili na žalbu organu za zaštitu podataka. Pošto su podaci vezani samo za anonimni ID, najlakše ih je obrisati opcijom u igri, opisanom gore.

Izmene politike

Ako se ova politika promeni, nova verzija biće objavljena na ovoj stranici sa novim datumom.
