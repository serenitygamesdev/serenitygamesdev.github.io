Royal Minesweeper

AndroidPuzzleLogic

The classic puzzle, refined: every board can be solved by pure logic. No coin flips, no 50/50 guesses — if you lose, it was a deduction you missed.

  • No-guess boards, checked by a built-in solver
  • Daily challenge — the same board for every player in the world
  • Beginner, Intermediate, Expert and custom boards up to 30×50
  • Stats, streaks and global leaderboards on Google Play Games
  • Unlockable Royal, Marble, Emerald and Obsidian themes

Neon Minesweeper

AndroidArcadePuzzle

Minesweeper turned into a fast arcade game. Clear board after board before the clock runs out, chain quick moves into combos and chase a new high score.

  • Race the clock — every cleared board adds time
  • Combo chains that multiply your points up to ×5
  • Mines cost time, not the game — the run goes on
  • Daily run and global leaderboards
  • Neon, Synthwave, Toxic and Inferno themes

Privacy Policy · Terms of Use · Support: app.mine.sweeper@gmail.com
